    09 — Questions

    Before you drop.

    Do I need a Shopify store?

    No. Fulmen has its own checkout. If you already run Shopify, connect it in two clicks and Fulmen syncs products and orders both ways.

    What happens when 20,000 people hit the drop at once?

    They join a fair queue. Every buyer gets a place in line and a held cart for five minutes, so nobody pays for an item that already sold out.

    Can my community posts really show on my site?

    Yes. Posts that tag your brand land in a review queue. Approve them and they appear on your drop page with credit to the creator.

    Which social channels does the scheduler support?

    Instagram, TikTok, Threads, Pinterest and X. The asset studio crops each look to the right ratio for each one.

    Can I leave whenever I want?

    Yes. Monthly plans cancel at any time, and you can export products, customers and orders as CSV whenever you like.
